Beatable Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Beatable does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The app fetches publicly available game catalog data and caches it locally on your device. Personal app state such as filters, favorites, hidden games, and your saved list is also stored locally on your device.

No account is required, and your personal state is not synced to any backend.

No third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks are included.
