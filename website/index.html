Frequently Asked Questions

What is Beatable?

Beatable is a free tool for browsing Xbox Game Pass and PlayStation Plus games by how long they take to beat. It pulls catalogue data and matches it against HowLongToBeat.

Do I need an account?

No. Beatable works immediately in your browser or after download on mobile. No sign-up, no email, no account of any kind - ever.

Where is my data stored?

All data and filters are processed locally. Nothing is sent to any server. It is a fully static and private experience.

Where can I use it?

You can use Beatable directly on the web at xatpy.github.io/beatable, and it is also available as an app for iPhone and Android devices.
