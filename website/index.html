Frequently Asked Questions

What is Beatable?

Beatable is a local-first game decision app for PlayStation Plus and Xbox Game Pass. It combines catalog browsing with personal on-device features like My Library, favorites, hidden games, and backlog status.

Do I need an account?

No. Beatable works immediately in your browser or after download on mobile. No sign-up, no email, no account of any kind - ever.

Where is my data stored?

Your personal state is stored locally in your browser or on your device. That includes filters, favorites, hidden games, and your saved list. No account is required.

Where can I use it?

You can use Beatable on the web at xatpy.github.io/beatable. It is also available on iPhone via the App Store. Android is currently in review and coming soon.
