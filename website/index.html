Frequently Asked Questions

What is Beatable?

Beatable is a local-first game decision app for PlayStation Plus and Xbox Game Pass. It combines catalog browsing with personal on-device features like My Library, favorites, hidden games, and backlog status.

Can I browse Game Pass and PlayStation Plus games by how long they take to beat?

Yes. Beatable lets you browse games by completion time, including Main Story, Main + Extra, and Completionist estimates.

Do I need an account?

No. Beatable works immediately in your browser or after download on mobile. No sign-up, no email, no account of any kind - ever.

Can I track my Game Pass or PlayStation Plus backlog without an account?

Yes. Beatable works as a simple game backlog tracker with saved games, favorites, completed titles, and hidden games, all stored locally on your device.

Where is my data stored?

Your personal state is stored locally in your browser or on your device. That includes filters, favorites, hidden games, and your saved list. No account is required.

Does Beatable work on web and iPhone?

Yes. You can use Beatable on the web, and it is also available on iPhone. Android is coming soon.

Where can I use it?

You can use Beatable on the web at xatpy.github.io/beatable. It is also available on iPhone via the App Store. Android is currently in review and coming soon.
